## 1. Scope of Representation (Limited Scope)

You are engaging the Firm for **limited-scope representation** delivered through
the Instant Attorney platform ("Platform"). Unless a separate written agreement
says otherwise, the engagement is limited to:

(a) AI-assisted intake to gather and organize the facts of your matter, conducted
under attorney supervision;

(b) analysis of the legal issues, risks, and possible pathways for your matter;
and

(c) preparation and attorney review of legal documents that the Firm determines
are appropriate and eligible for this service.

**This engagement does NOT include**, unless separately agreed in writing and
separately priced: court appearances; filing documents with any court or agency
on your behalf; representation in litigation, hearings, mediation, or arbitration;
negotiation with opposing parties on your behalf; deadlines, statutes of
limitation, or calendaring; ongoing monitoring of your matter; or representation
in any matter outside the Firm's licensed jurisdictions (Texas and Illinois).

You remain responsible for any deadline unless and until the Firm expressly agrees
in writing to assume responsibility for it. The limited scope is reasonable under
the circumstances, and you consent to it. [ATTORNEY REVIEW: confirm scope
boundaries comply with Tex. Disciplinary R. 1.02(b).]

## 2. Phases of Service; When Representation Begins

- **Phase I (free):** General legal information only. **No attorney-client
  relationship is formed, and no attorney-client privilege applies** to Phase I.
- **Phase II (subscription):** The attorney-client relationship is formed when you
  sign this Agreement and your subscription is active. Intake conducted after you
  sign is undertaken within that relationship.
- **Phase III (consult):** A one-time live consultation, separately priced.

The Firm's acceptance of this engagement is contingent on completion of a
conflict-of-interest check (Section 9). The Firm may decline or terminate the
engagement if a conflict or other professional obligation requires it.

## 3. AI-Assisted Services and Attorney Supervision

You acknowledge and consent that the Firm uses artificial intelligence tools
(including models provided by Anthropic, PBC) to assist with intake, analysis, and
document drafting. AI is a tool used under the supervision of a licensed attorney;
it is not itself your attorney and does not exercise legal judgment.

**A licensed attorney reviews AI-generated documents before they are delivered to
you as attorney-approved.** Drafts shown to you before attorney approval are
clearly marked as pre-review drafts and **must not be filed, signed, sent, or
relied upon** until an attorney approves them. The Firm's use of AI is intended to
comply with the Texas Disciplinary Rules of Professional Conduct and Texas Ethics
Opinion 705. Your separate **Consent to AI-Assisted Services** (Document 2) is
incorporated by reference.

## 4. Fees, Costs, and Automatic Usage Charges

You agree to the following fees for legal services. **These are legal fees for the
services described and are intended to be reasonable under Tex. Disciplinary R.
1.04.** [ATTORNEY REVIEW: confirm fee characterization and reasonableness.]

**(a) Subscription.** A recurring fee of **$9.99 per month**, billed in advance,
which renews automatically each month until cancelled.

**(b) Automatic usage top-ups.** Document drafting and analysis consume AI
processing capacity that costs the Firm money. The Platform meters your AI usage
in dollars. **When your cumulative metered usage since your last top-up reaches
$4.75, you authorize the Firm to automatically charge a one-time $8.50 usage
top-up** to your payment method on file. This may occur multiple times in a
billing period if your usage warrants it. Top-up amounts and the trigger threshold
are stated on the Platform and may be changed prospectively with notice.

**(c) Your monthly spending limit.** You set a pre-approved monthly cap on
automatic top-ups (default **$25 per month**). The Firm will not exceed your cap
automatically; if a top-up would exceed it, automatic charging pauses and AI
features pause until you raise your cap or the next month begins. You may change
your cap at any time on the Platform.

**(d) Pause on failed payment.** If a charge is declined, AI features are paused
until payment succeeds; your file remains accessible subject to Section 7.

**(e) Cancellation true-up.** If you cancel, you authorize a final charge for any
metered usage already incurred but not yet charged (Section 7).

**(f) Other services.** Fees for services beyond this limited scope (e.g.,
litigation, court appearances, the Phase III consult) are separately disclosed and
separately agreed.

You authorize the Firm and its payment processor (Stripe) to store your payment
method and to charge the fees above without further authorization for each charge.
The Firm does not charge a contingent fee under this Agreement.

## 5. Billing, Renewal, and Cancellation

Your subscription renews automatically each month. **You may cancel at any time**
through the Platform or by contacting the Firm. On cancellation: (i) automatic
renewal stops; (ii) you retain access through the end of the period you have
already paid for; (iii) **fees already paid are non-refundable and are not
prorated**; and (iv) the cancellation true-up in Section 4(e) applies. See the
Billing and Refund Disclosure (Document 5).

## 6. Client Responsibilities

You agree to: provide truthful, accurate, and complete information; respond to the
Firm's requests promptly; review pre-approval drafts and not rely on them until
approved; use the Platform only for lawful purposes; and not use the Firm's
services to commit or further any crime or fraud, or to plan future unlawful
conduct. Inaccurate or incomplete information may materially affect the quality of
the services and the documents produced.

## 7. File Retention, Archival, and Destruction

The Firm maintains your case file for the duration of the engagement. **Following
cancellation or prolonged inactivity, your file is archived and then permanently
deleted [30 days] after archival.** Before deletion, you are responsible for
downloading or exporting any documents you wish to keep, and the Firm will provide
[notice and an export option] before deletion. After deletion, the Firm cannot
recover your file.

## 8. Confidentiality and Attorney-Client Privilege

The Firm protects your confidential information under Tex. Disciplinary R. 1.05.
Communications you make to the Firm to obtain legal services within Phase II are
intended to be protected by attorney-client privilege, **subject to important
limits**, including the crime-fraud exception and loss of privilege if you disclose
communications to third parties. The Firm's use of vendors acting as its agents
under confidentiality obligations is not intended to waive privilege. Privilege is
a legal doctrine applied by courts; the Firm cannot guarantee how any particular
court will rule. See the ACP Notice (Document 2).

## 9. Conflicts of Interest

The Firm checks for conflicts of interest under Tex. Disciplinary R. 1.06–1.09. If
a conflict exists or arises, the Firm may be unable to begin or continue the
engagement and will handle the matter consistent with its professional
obligations.

## 10. No Guarantee of Outcome

The Firm will provide competent, diligent representation within the limited scope,
but **does not and cannot guarantee any particular result or outcome.** Statements
about the law or your matter are professional assessments, not promises.

## 11. Jurisdiction Limits

The Firm is licensed only in **Texas and Illinois**. If your matter arises under
the law of, or must be handled in, another jurisdiction, the Firm may decline the
matter, limit its services to general analysis, or help you find appropriate local
counsel.

## 12. Termination and Withdrawal

You may terminate this Agreement at any time. The Firm may withdraw consistent with
Tex. Disciplinary R. 1.15 (including for nonpayment, lack of cooperation, or
ethical reasons). Termination does not affect fees already earned or charges
already authorized, and Sections 7, 8, 10, and 13–15 survive.

## 13. Communications and Electronic Delivery

You consent to electronic communications and to delivery of documents and notices
through the Platform and by email. You are responsible for maintaining a current
email address and for safeguarding your account credentials.
