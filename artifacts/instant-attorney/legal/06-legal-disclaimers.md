## Appendix — Suggested landing-page softenings (advertising compliance)
To reduce overpromising risk, consider revising absolute claims such as:
- "**protected by privilege — not just encrypted**" and "**the strongest legal
  protection available**" → frame as *intended to be* privileged, subject to limits.
- "**A court order cannot compel your attorney to disclose privileged
  communications**" → privilege has **exceptions** (e.g., crime-fraud) and is decided
  by courts; avoid absolute phrasing.
- "**likely privileged**" → acceptable, but pair with the limits link.
- Any "48-hour" promise → state it as a **target**, with eligibility limits.
- Ensure the Phase II price tile discloses **automatic usage top-ups, the spending
  cap, no-refund policy, and data deletion on cancellation** (now reflected on the
  site).
