This Privacy Policy explains how **Crawford Law PLLC** ("Firm," "we," "us")
collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use the Instant
Attorney platform (the "Platform").

**Note on confidentiality:** For Phase II clients, the information you share is also
**confidential client information** protected by the attorney-client privilege and
the Firm's duty of confidentiality (Tex. Disciplinary R. 1.05). Where this Policy
and those duties differ, the **stricter protection** applies.

## 2. Information We Collect

**You provide:**
- **Account & identity:** name, email, electronic-signature name, and login
  credentials.
- **Intake & case content:** the facts, messages, goals, and documents you submit;
  uploaded files/attachments and any text extracted from them.
- **Communications:** messages with the Firm and support requests.

**Collected automatically:**
- **Usage data:** features used, AI usage metering (in dollars/tokens), top-up and
  spending-limit activity, timestamps.
- **Device/log data:** IP address, browser/device type, and similar technical data,
  including via essential cookies needed to run the Platform and keep you signed in.

**From service providers:**
- **Payment data:** processed by **Stripe**. We do **not** store full card numbers;
  Stripe provides us limited information (e.g., card brand/last four, status).

## 3. How We Use Information
- To provide Phase I information and Phase II/III legal services, including AI-
  assisted intake, analysis, and attorney-reviewed documents.
- To create and manage your account and case file.
- To meter usage and process subscription fees, automatic top-ups, spending-limit
  enforcement, and the cancellation true-up.
- To communicate with you (e.g., document-ready notifications, billing notices).
- To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal and ethical
  obligations (including conflicts checks and records duties).

## 4. AI Processing
We use AI providers (including **Anthropic, PBC**) to process your content to
deliver the services. We configure AI processing to: use providers under
confidentiality and data-processing terms; request **zero data retention** by the
AI provider where that option is available; and **prohibit use of your content to
train AI models.** AI output is reviewed by an attorney before being delivered as
approved. See the AI Consent (Document 2).

## 5. How We Share Information
We share information only as needed to provide the service or as required by law:
- **Service providers (our agents):** AI inference (**Anthropic**), cloud hosting
  and storage (**Supabase**), payment processing (**Stripe**), and transactional
  email (**Resend**), each under contractual confidentiality and data-protection
  obligations.
- **Legal and ethical compliance:** where required by law, court order, or the Texas
  Disciplinary Rules, including the **crime-fraud** limitation on privilege.
- **With your direction or consent.**
- **Business changes:** in connection with a merger or transfer of the practice,
  consistent with our professional obligations to protect client information.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for behavioral
advertising.**

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
- We retain your case file for the duration of your engagement.
- **After cancellation or prolonged inactivity, your file is archived and then
  permanently deleted [30 days] after archival.** Export anything you wish to keep
  before deletion; after deletion we cannot recover it.
- Certain records (e.g., conflicts-check data, billing/tax records, records we are
  ethically or legally required to keep) may be retained for longer as required.

## 7. Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards
appropriate to the sensitivity of legal information, including access controls,
encryption in transit, and row-level access restrictions in our database. **No
system is perfectly secure**, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Notify us
immediately if you believe your account has been compromised. In the event of a
data breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by applicable
law.
