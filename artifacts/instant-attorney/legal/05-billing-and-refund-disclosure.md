## How automatic usage top-ups work
- The Platform meters the cost of the AI you use as you generate and analyze
  documents.
- When that running total reaches **$4.75**, an **$8.50** top-up is charged
  automatically to your card on file.
- This protects against interruptions mid-work. It can recur whenever you reach the
  threshold again.
- **You stay in control with your monthly cap.** Once automatic top-ups would exceed
  your cap (default **$25**), we stop charging automatically and pause AI features
  until you raise the cap or the next month begins. We warn you as you approach the
  cap.
- If a charge is **declined**, AI features pause until you update your card and
  payment succeeds.

## Renewal, cancellation, and refunds
- Your subscription **renews automatically every month** until you cancel.
- **You can cancel anytime** on the Platform (Account → Billing) or by contacting us
  at [contact]. Cancellation is simple and does not require talking to anyone.
- When you cancel: automatic renewal stops; you keep access **through the end of the
  period you already paid for**; **fees already paid are non-refundable and are not
  prorated** (except where a refund is required by law).
- **Cancellation true-up:** at cancellation, you authorize a final charge for AI
  usage you already incurred that has not yet been charged.

## What happens to your data when you cancel
Your interactive access ends after the paid period. The Firm then **retains your
client file** under its Retention Policy (Document 7) — generally at least five years,
longer for certain records — and you may **request a copy** during that period.
**Please export anything you want to keep before your access ends.**
